# Appendix G scene specifications — Group A (S03–S10)

Status: task/asset specifications plus private candidate USD builds and layout-0
render audits. These are local reconstructions from the task text and the
paper's small Appendix G(a) reference images, not recovered paper geometry or
coordinates. The paper says it ran two rollouts per task by varying object
locations; exact poses and scoring thresholds are not given. A rendered USD is
not automatically an accepted or inference-ready task: see the per-task audit
below and do not use tasks marked `NOT INFERENCE-READY` for benchmark rollouts.

## Shared visual and licensing rules

- Use the existing DROID-style wooden table/camera frame. The paper images
  repeatedly show a thin dark rectangular outline on the tabletop; reproduce it
  as a visual-only black-tape target mark when visible, but do not call it an
  official target unless the local task explicitly adopts that assumption.
- Keep the paper screenshots as external references only; do not copy them into
  the repository. Use two deterministic, paired object layouts, not random
  scene changes.
- Reuse already-bundled YCB models under
  `assets/third_party/ycb/downloads/` where listed below. YCB data is CC BY 4.0;
  retain attribution, source URL, license URL, and modifications in notices and
  the asset manifest. CC BY allows redistribution/adaptation with attribution.
- New project-authored fixtures/proxies should be placed in USD and explicitly
  released under CC0-1.0 with a per-asset notice. Never label a third-party
  model or its derivative CC0 just because it is included in a CC0-authored
  scene.
- Keep each downloaded file below 25 MB. Poly Haven's pages show much larger
  default packages for the basket (42.89 MB) and cloth (194.6 MB); select only
  low-resolution GLB/USD or 1K image maps and verify byte size before keeping
  them. If the small variant is unavailable or the shape is a poor match, use
  the specified CC0 project-authored fallback instead.

## S03 — Remove the pair of gloves from the open drawer and put it on the table

- **Scene and initial state:** wooden tabletop; shallow wooden drawer/tray near
  the back-center with its opening facing the robot; dark interior and a black
  pull on the front; one visually paired set of yellow work gloves resting
  inside the open cavity. Leave a clear tabletop drop zone beside the drawer,
  aligned with the dark rectangular outline seen in the reference. The task
  requests removal from an already-open drawer, so the drawer can be a static
  open fixture; no drawer articulation is needed for this instruction.
- **Approximate proxy size:** drawer exterior about 0.28 × 0.18 × 0.08 m, with
  an interior around 0.24 × 0.14 m; glove pair about 0.16 × 0.10 × 0.03 m.
  Leave the cuff/edge graspable and a clear 0.22 × 0.22 m table drop zone.
- **Gloves source:** “work gloves” by Aleksandr Sagidullin,
  [Sketchfab model page](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/work-gloves-991e775b6b5b4fdab682af56d08bb119),
  page license **CC Attribution / CC BY 4.0**;
  [license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Download as a
  Sketchfab glTF package and convert to USD; the source page lists 14.4k
  triangles. Attribution and a note for scaling/recoloring are required.
  **Redistribution:** allowed under CC BY 4.0 with attribution and change notice.
- **Suitability gate/fallback:** verify that the downloadable mesh is a pair
  and visually resembles the yellow pair in the paper image; if it is one glove,
  unsuitable, or its package exceeds 25 MB, create two simple low-poly glove
  meshes as one graspable paired proxy in CC0 USD. Do not use the page's model
  until its downloadable package and attribution are recorded.
- **Drawer:** project-authored USD: floor, three interior walls, front panel,
  black pull; static collider, open top/front. CC0-1.0 notice; redistributable.
- **Evaluator gap:** current `remove_and_place` only compares the gloves with
  the second listed object (the drawer), so it rewards proximity to the source
  drawer instead of checking “out of drawer and on table.” It cannot score this
  task semantically. A future evaluator must check glove geometry outside the
  drawer volume and stable support on the table/drop zone.

## S04 — Put the marker on table

- **Scene and initial state:** bare wooden table, the white-bodied/red-capped
  marker shown in the reference, and the recurring dark rectangular tabletop
  outline. For a nontrivial local proxy, place the marker outside that outlined
  zone and define the zone as the destination on the table. This zone is a
  declared reconstruction assumption: the prompt only says “on table,” and the
  reference image itself appears to show the marker already standing on the
  table. Do not claim the zone interpretation is the paper's official setup.
- **Approximate proxy size:** a 0.20 × 0.20 m marked zone and YCB-scaled marker
  around 0.13–0.15 m long; these dimensions are project choices, not paper
  measurements.
- **Marker asset:** already-bundled
  `assets/third_party/ycb/downloads/040_large_marker_google_16k.tgz`; extract
  its textured OBJ/MTL/texture and scale/orient to the reference. Source:
  [official YCB model/download index](https://ycb-benchmarks.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html),
  dataset license **CC BY 4.0**;
  [license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). No new download
  is needed. **Redistribution:** allowed with YCB attribution and a note for
  any recolor/scale changes.
- **Fallback:** if the scanned marker color/shape does not match the visible
  white barrel/red cap, author a simple two-piece marker in CC0 USD; retain the
  YCB archive as the preferred licensed source only if its visible geometry
  actually fits.
- **Evaluator gap:** current `place_on_surface` checks table bounds plus over
  20 cm displacement, not whether the marker is placed in the visible zone or
  released stably. Since the prompt/photo do not establish a unique destination
  or a nontrivial initial state, this task needs a documented local convention
  before inference; otherwise report it as non-evaluable rather than a paper
  success/failure.

## S05 — Pick up the apple and put it in the basket

- **Scene and initial state:** open woven basket near back-center, red apple on
  the right/front outside the basket, and a yellow banana to the left as the
  visible distractor. This composition follows the Appendix image; object
  positions are local assumptions. Make the basket cavity visibly open and large
  enough for one apple.
- **Approximate proxy size:** basket outside about 0.24 × 0.18 × 0.10 m, with
  interior clearance for a 0.07–0.09 m apple; banana distractor about 0.16 m
  long. Scale the source basket down from its listed 0.4 m width.
- **Apple:** already-bundled
  `assets/third_party/ycb/downloads/013_apple_google_16k.tgz`, textured YCB
  Google 16k scan (OBJ/MTL/texture after extraction), CC BY 4.0 via the
  [official YCB index](https://ycb-benchmarks.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html) and
  [CC BY license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Reuse it as-is
  where possible; redistribution is permitted with attribution/change notice.
- **Basket:** [Poly Haven Wicker Basket 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/wicker_basket_01),
  creator Kuutti Siitonen, **CC0-1.0**; its page offers glTF and USD and describes
  a 0.4 m basket with a tight rectangular weave. Use the reduced-resolution
  package, scale to tabletop size, and tint pink/red only if needed to match the
  reference. [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license) explicitly
  allows redistribution. **Redistribution:** allowed under CC0; retaining
  provenance is still recommended.
- **Banana distractor:** no local banana archive exists. Optional source is
  YCB 011 banana from the same official index, CC BY 4.0; download only if the
  archive is under 25 MB. Deterministic fallback is a project-authored curved
  yellow banana mesh in CC0 USD.
- **Fallback:** if the small Wicker Basket 01 package is unavailable/too large
  or the woven model is visually too unlike the pink plastic basket in the
  reference, use a project-authored open rectangular basket with repeated
  lattice bars, pink/red material, thin walls, and a dark interior collider in
  CC0 USD.
- **Evaluator gap:** current `pick_and_place` uses source/target center
  distance; it can mark an apple outside but near the basket as success. Check
  apple center/bounds inside the basket's inner footprint, above the basket
  floor and below its rim, and settled before success.

## S06 — Put the towel on the white cup

- **Scene and initial state:** folded white towel on the right/front; white cup
  on the left/back; a tall purple bottle/cup-shaped item is visible behind the
  towel in the paper image but its identity is unclear. If retaining that visual
  distractor, make it a static, generic purple cylinder and do not attach task
  semantics to it. The towel starts beside the cup; intended final relation is
  towel resting across the cup top.
- **Approximate proxy size:** folded towel about 0.14 × 0.10 × 0.025 m; cup
  about 0.08 m across and 0.10 m high. Ensure the towel can rest across the rim
  without clipping through it.
- **Cup:** already-bundled YCB 025 mug archive
  `assets/third_party/ycb/downloads/025_mug_google_16k.tgz`, textured OBJ/MTL
  after extraction; use a white material. YCB CC BY 4.0, with source and license
  at the [official YCB index](https://ycb-benchmarks.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html)
  and [CC BY deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Redistribution
  is permitted with attribution and modification notice.
- **Towel:** use a project-authored folded, white terrycloth-shaped USD mesh
  with a thin box collision proxy (CC0-1.0; redistributable). Optional visual
  texture: [Poly Haven Terry Cloth](https://polyhaven.com/a/terry_cloth),
  **CC0-1.0**, image-map formats; download only selected 1K maps, not its
  194.6 MB default package. The blue source texture may be whitened/recolored;
  record that modification. Poly Haven's [license](https://polyhaven.com/license)
  permits redistribution.
- **Fallback:** if texture maps exceed 25 MB or add little at camera scale, use
  a white procedural folded-cloth surface with shallow ridges and no external
  texture. Keep the object rigid for this simulator proxy and disclose that it
  is not deformable cloth.
- **Evaluator gap:** current `place_on_object` is a broad center-distance test;
  it does not check towel-on-cup support, footprint overlap, or settling. Use
  vertical support/contact and overlap tests. Because the towel is rigidified,
  do not claim cloth-manipulation fidelity.

## S09 — Put the pair of scissors into the drawer

- **Scene and initial state:** shallow wooden drawer near the back-center,
  open cavity facing the robot and a black front pull; blue-handled scissors
  on the front/right tabletop. The image also shows a white/red marker at
  front/left; include it as a distractor using the same YCB 040 marker as S04.
  Make the drawer cavity long enough for the scissors to lie flat, with the
  interior floor visible and a small clearance below the lip.
- **Approximate proxy size:** drawer interior about 0.24 × 0.13 × 0.06 m; orient
  its long axis toward the scissors so the approximately 0.20 m scanned model
  can lie flat inside.
- **Scissors:** reuse existing local YCB 037 `google_16k/textured.obj`, `.mtl`,
  and texture under `assets/third_party/ycb/037_scissors/`; currently the scene
  adaptation recolors the handles blue. Source is the
  [official YCB index](https://ycb-benchmarks.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html),
  dataset license CC BY 4.0, [license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Redistribution/adaptation is allowed with attribution and a blue-recolor
  change notice.
- **Drawer:** project-authored static USD open tray/drawer with wood panels,
  open top/front, black pull, and simple collision shapes; CC0-1.0 and
  redistributable. A static open fixture is sufficient because the instruction
  says to place scissors in it, not to open/close it.
- **Evaluator gap:** current `place_in_drawer` is not implemented and returns
  `success: null`. Add an oriented drawer-interior volume test: scissors mesh
  bounds entirely within the horizontal cavity footprint, below the rim and
  above the drawer floor, followed by a stable-settle check. A center-distance
  test alone is insufficient.

- **Scene and initial state:** folded white towel near the front/left; open
  woven basket behind it toward the left/back. The reference also shows a large
  bright-pink net/fabric-looking object at right, but its identity is not clear
